# Sources

Research date: 19 November 2026 (launch day). The game released 19 Nov 2026
on PlayStation 5.

## The official trophy list

- Exophase, "Grand Theft Auto VI PSN trophies":
  https://www.exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi-psn/trophies/
  Every official name, description and grade, frozen 18 Nov 2026.
- PSNProfiles, "Grand Theft Auto VI trophies":
  https://psnprofiles.com/trophies/43531-grand-theft-auto-vi
  Grades and PSN progress targets. The two lists agree on every name,
  description and grade: 55 trophies, 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver,
  46 bronze, 28 hidden.

  https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/gta-6-trophy-guide-platinum-roadmap-all-55-trophies/
  Per-trophy unlock methods, the three-stage roadmap (60-80h story,

  https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-trophy-list/

  E3/E4, the Mile Zero tank location, the 500-action Criminal Profile target,
  the time-sensitive Race Against Yago, and the five hidden trophies with no
  confirmed method yet.

## What is still unconfirmed at launch

Five hidden trophies have no confirmed unlock method in any published guide
as of 19 Nov 2026: In The Beginning..., Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess,
The Truth Is Out There, and Ten Pin (method still being confirmed). This
guide marks each of them unconfirmed in its own entry rather than inventing
a method. The Chapter 1 balcony meditation's exact role in Positive Mental
Attitude is likewise unconfirmed; the guide tells the reader to do it anyway
and keep a Chapter 1 save.

## How sources are cited in the app

The app itself carries no outbound links (it must work with the network off),
so each trophy's guide names its sources as text: the official list
(Exophase + PSNProfiles) for its name, description and grade, and KeenGamer
and/or Output Lag for its method. The footer of every page states the same
in one place.
