Read this before you start

  • GTA VI has no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Save by hand at the start of every chapter, and keep one untouched save from right before the final mission.
  • Positive Mental Attitude is the single trophy you can miss. Do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1. It never comes back.
  • Cheats turn off trophies and saving on that save file. Leave them off your trophy save.
  • Five hidden trophies still have no confirmed method. Their cards say so outright. Do not grind blindly for them.

    The 100% checklist

    The Gold trophy A Long Road needs 100% completion. The in-game checklist shows up under Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion, once the story is done. These are the parts of it the launch guides confirm:

    • Finish the story, all five chapters.
    • 300 stunts. The trophy stops at 250, the checklist wants 50 more.
    • 70 of the 80 challenges.
    • 200 of the 277 base vehicle models discovered.
    • All 8 bank vaults robbed.
    • All 39 illegal operations robbed.
    • Work you do before the story ends still counts.
